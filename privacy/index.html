Privacy Policy

Agent World (TikTok app: Mo Video Poster) · VD Films (@vdfilms-uk) · Effective 20 November 2026

Agent World is a private tool used only by the owner of the VD Films account to manage their own videos. It is not offered to anyone else. This policy explains what it does with TikTok data.

1. What the app reads from TikTok

When the owner logs in with TikTok, the app reads, for the owner’s own account only:

  • Profile information: display name, username and profile picture.
  • Account statistics: follower count, likes and number of videos.
  • The owner’s video list with each video’s view, like and comment counts.

This is shown on a private dashboard that only the owner sees. The app does not read information about viewers, followers or any other account.

2. What the app sends to TikTok

Videos the owner has made and approved are uploaded to the owner’s TikTok inbox as drafts. The owner reviews and posts them in the TikTok app; the app never posts publicly by itself.

3. Where data is stored

Login tokens, statistics and posting records are stored only on the owner’s own Mac. There is no public database or cloud copy.

4. Sharing

No data is sold, rented or shared with third parties, and none of it is used for advertising or profiling.

5. Keeping and deleting data

Tokens are kept until they expire or the app is disconnected; statistics and posting records are kept for the owner’s own use and can be deleted at any time. To disconnect, open TikTok → Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions.

6. Children

The app is not meant for children and does not handle children’s information.
