Privacy Policy

Mo Video Poster · Effective 20 November 2026

Mo Video Poster (“the app”) is a personal tool its owner uses to post their own videos to their own TikTok account. This policy explains what information the app handles and why.

1. Information the app receives

When the owner signs in with TikTok, TikTok gives the app:

  • Basic account details, such as the account’s ID and display name, so the app knows which account it is posting to.
  • Access tokens that let the app post videos on the owner’s behalf, limited to the permissions approved on TikTok’s sign-in screen.
  • The owner’s own account statistics: follower, like and video counts, and the view counts of the owner’s own public videos.

The app does not receive or store passwords, and it does not collect information about individual viewers, followers or anyone else.

2. How it is used

This information is used only to upload and publish videos the owner has approved, and to show the owner their own progress (for example, follower and view totals) on a private dashboard on their own computer. It is not used for advertising or profiling, and it is never shared.

3. Where it is stored

Tokens and posting records are stored only on the owner’s own computer. The app does not run a public database, and it does not sell, rent or share this information with anyone. Videos are sent only to TikTok, through TikTok’s official API.

4. How long it is kept

Tokens are kept until they expire or the app is disconnected. Posting records (which video went out, and when) are kept for the owner’s own records and can be deleted at any time.

5. Your choices

You can disconnect the app at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions. Once it is disconnected, its tokens stop working and are deleted from the owner’s computer.

6. Children

The app is not meant for children and does not knowingly handle children’s information.

This policy may be updated. The date at the top shows the latest version.
